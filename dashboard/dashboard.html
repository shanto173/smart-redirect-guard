Trusted sites

What trusting a site does

Trusting a site makes SRG leave that site alone, but only for the layers you choose. Nothing is switched off silently.

  • New tabs: SRG will not close tabs that the site opens. Other sites are unaffected.
  • Redirects: SRG will not block, flag or offer to undo redirects on the site.
  • Element blocking: SRG will not hide anything on the site's pages.

Any layer you leave unticked keeps protecting you on that site. Trust applies to the domain and its subdomains. Trusting never turns off protection on other websites.
