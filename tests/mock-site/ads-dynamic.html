Advertisements injected after the page loads

Test 8 — a one-off page-load scan would miss every one of these.

Three advertisements will be injected on a timer. A countdown is shown below. This mimics sites that load their content first and inject advertising seconds later.

Waiting...

Expected: each injected advertisement is detected by the MutationObserver and hidden within roughly a second of appearing — the observer debounces for about 150 ms and runs its batch in idle time. The counter in the popup rises each time. Nothing else on the page is affected.
